## 1. Vision & Core Concept

**Cashflow** is an **offline-first personal finance tracker** that enables deliberate cash-flow management through a "book of transactions" model. It does **not** sync with banks or fetch real data—instead, users manually log transactions to maintain privacy and control.

### Core Promise
Tell users their **true usable balance** = physical money held − money locked for goals − money reserved for monthly budgets.

### Why This Matters
Most expense apps show spending vs. budget. Cashflow goes deeper: it shows how much money is *actually available to spend right now* after accounting for:
- Physical accounts (Bank, Cash)
- Locked funds reserved for savings goals (sinking funds)
- Budgeted amounts reserved for monthly categories
