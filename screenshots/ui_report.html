📋 1. Executive Summary & Design System Review
Foundations

Cashflow adheres strictly to an offline-first, zero-overhead personal finance architecture. Visual styling uses Material Design 3 tokens encapsulated in AppColors, AppTypography, and AppSpacing. The visual signature is anchored around the Usable Balance Hero Card, differentiating raw physical bank holdings from actual disposable liquidity.

Semantic Palette Consistency: Green (#10b981) for Usable Balance and Income; Amber (#F59E0B) for locked sinking funds; Rose (#EF4444) for over-budget warnings; and Blue for informational limits.
Dark Mode Elevation: Avoids pure #000000 OLED black in favor of deep midnight blue/slate (#090D16 canvas with #111827 cards), retaining card contouring and subtle border contrast.

🛡️ 4. Scope Guardrails & Offline Architecture
Scope Guard
1. Strict Offline-First Principle

No cloud accounts, no mandatory internet connection, no analytics telemetry. All data resides in local SQLite (cashflow.db) with direct user-initiated JSON/CSV exports.

2. Usable Balance as Core Anchor

The central formula is always: Usable = Physical - Locked Goals. Monthly budgets remain strictly tracking ceilings, never balance reductions.

3. Low Cognitive Friction (≤ 2 Taps)

No complex multi-currency exchange or double-entry book-keeping overhead. Actions like logging an expense or locking funds must complete within two taps.
